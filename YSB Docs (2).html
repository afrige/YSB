Server rules

Keep it friendly and everyone has a good time.

  1. Be kind. No harassment, hate speech or bullying.
  2. No spam. Don't flood channels, repeat messages or mass-ping people.
  3. Use the right channel. Check what a channel is for before you post in it.
  4. Keep it clean. No NSFW or graphic content.
  5. Protect privacy. Don't share personal info, yours or anyone else's.
  6. Don't pretend to be staff. Only Emilia and the mods speak for the server.
  7. Listen to the mods. If a mod asks you to stop, stop. Disagree in a message, not in the chat.

Tips

A few things that make the server nicer for everyone.

  • Say hi. A quick hello in the text channels is the easiest way to meet people.
  • Hop into voice. There are 2 voice channels if you'd rather talk than type.
  • Mute what you don't need. Turn off notifications for channels you don't follow.
  • See a problem? Message a mod instead of replying to it.
  • Bring a friend. Share the invite link at the bottom of this page.

Questions

Who do I message if I need help?

Any of the team. Send a message to Emilia, akoge, specter or Omorfos78 and one of them will get back to you.

Can I invite my friends?

Yes. Copy the invite link from the bottom of this page and send it to them.

Can I advertise my own server?

Only if a mod says yes first. See the Advertising section above.

Why are some mods called trial mods?

akoge, specter and Omorfos78 are still on a trial period. Emilia is the owner and has the final say.
